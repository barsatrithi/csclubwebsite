Our Mission

Make computer science more welcoming, practical, and creative on campus.

Learn in public

From beginner-friendly workshops to deeper technical sessions, we want students to feel comfortable asking questions and exploring new tools.

Build with others

The club is a place for collaboration, side projects, and shared momentum, whether you are just starting out or already building independently.

Support the community

We want to connect students to tutoring, peer guidance, and useful resources that make academic and career growth more accessible.
